## Findings already recorded (pinned GreptimeDB v1.2.1)

- `path`/`release` must be backtick-quoted in DDL and queries (double quotes are strings over MySQL protocol) (ADR 0028, backticks).
- `DELETE` on an append-mode table is rejected (ADR 0029).
- Flow with `GROUP BY` and no aggregate works for distinct hour tables; `SELECT DISTINCT … GROUP BY` fails (ADR 0041).
- Flow sink tables get `update_at` and keys on non-time group columns (ADR 0036).

## Verification (`scripts/release-check.sh`)

7 ACs. Bugs found by running the real thing and fixed in this release: default per-key rate limit of
600/min rejected 41 % of a busy site's batches (now 60 000/min); a lagging fresh lane did not hand its backlog
to the 5 000-row backfill lane (now `fresh_lag_secs`); dashboard defects visible only when rendered (empty
Sessions, day-index retention, step series in the funnel chart, wrong Prometheus target field, empty Session
detail).

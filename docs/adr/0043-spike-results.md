# ADR 0043 — Spike results

Status: Accepted

## Context

Numbers from the spike runs.

## Decision

Local single-node GreptimeDB v1.2.1 in Docker on Apple silicon. See the table below (filled by `greprum spike`).

Dataset: **1 000 007 events**, site `spike`, 7 days, 5 000 visitors, generated by `greprum gen --seed 7`
(5 runs per query, `greprum spike`). Single-node GreptimeDB v1.2.1, Docker on Apple silicon.

| Query | p95 (ms) | Gate (ms) | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| rollup overview (10 queries) | 180 | 2 000 | yes |
| rollup top pages | 43 | 2 000 | yes |
| rollup journeys | 16 | 2 000 | yes |
| funnel (3 steps, 30 min window) | 46 | 10 000 | yes |
| retention (7 days) | 46 | 10 000 | yes |
| sessions, last 24 h | 20 | 10 000 | yes |
| `count(DISTINCT visitor_id)` raw | 12 | 10 000 | yes |

Ingest: 1 M events inserted by the debug build of `greprum gen` through `SqlxSink` multi-row
inserts in about 4 minutes (batches of 5 000 rows; the generator, not the database, was the limit).

## Consequences

1 M events pass every gate with a wide margin. The 50 M-event run was descoped by the owner (2026-10-12); `make spike N=<events>` repeats the measurement at any size. Funnel and sessions are the queries expected to grow fastest.
